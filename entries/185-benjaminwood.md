Page 185 is very sensible. Operators are just methods. 10 / 3 is 3. 10.div 3 is also 3. Nobody shouts.

Then @benjaminwood came by with a red pen:

His profile says "Extinguished Engineer". So all day he puts out fires, and then he lights a small weird one in the margin.

10 / 3 is 3. The leftover 1 ran off with the foxes. It is weird now. It is very happy.
