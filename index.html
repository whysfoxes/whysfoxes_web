why's FOXES

a guest book that goes to Ruby events

A real paperback of why's (poignant) Guide to Ruby gets carried from event to event. People open it to any page they like and write on it. Then the pages with something for everyone get scanned, optimized and posted, so the book can be in two places at once. Mostly it is in a backpack. Only messages to the community get posted, so no "thanks for coming to my talk" and no "use my library" pages.
